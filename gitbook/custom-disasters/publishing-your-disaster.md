# Publishing Your Disaster

Once you have finished your custom disaster, you can use it in game with:

```text
/disasters start <custom disaster> [level] [player]
```

If `natural` is enabled in the settings skeleton body, the disaster can naturally occur on players in worlds where custom disasters are enabled.

## Submitting Your Disaster To The Catalog

To get your custom disaster published to the catalog, submit it through the Discord server in the disaster submissions channel.

After review, accepted disasters can be posted to the catalog. The in-game catalog updates live so servers can browse and install community disasters.
